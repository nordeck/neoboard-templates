## 🚀 1-2-4-All: How it works in 4 steps

**🎯 Purpose:** Include every voice and get from many individual ideas to a few strong ones in a short time.

### 1. Alone (1 min)

- **Action:** Ask the question. Everyone reflects in silence and notes ideas in their own box.

### 2. Pairs (2 min)

- **Action:** Pairs share their ideas and develop them further in their pair box.

### 3. Foursomes (4 min)

- **Action:** Two pairs form a foursome. What is similar, what differs? Choose the strongest idea.

### 4. All (5 min)

- **Action:** Each group presents its most important idea and places it in the results area.
- **Rule:** Only share new ideas, skip duplicates.

## 🛠️ Your role as facilitator

- **Pacemaker:** Stick to the short time boxes.
- **Group size:** With more than eight people, duplicate the frame.
