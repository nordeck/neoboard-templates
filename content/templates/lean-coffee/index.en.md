## 🚀 Lean Coffee: How it works in 4 steps

**🎯 Purpose:** A meeting without a fixed agenda. The group decides what matters most.

### 1. Collect topics (3–5 min)

- **Action:** Everyone writes topic ideas on cards (add topic to topic collection).
- **Rule:** One topic per card. Short and clear.
- **Goal:** All cards in "Topic Collection".

### 2. Prioritise with dot voting (3 min)

- **Action:** Everyone gets a fixed number of votes (e.g. 3 dots).
- **Rule:** Votes can be distributed freely, even all on one topic.
- **Goal:** Sort the top cards into "Topic Selection". This is your agenda.

### 3. Discuss with timeboxing (core phase)

- **Action:** Move the top topic to "Topic Discussion" and start a 5-minute timer.
- **The pitch:** The topic owner explains it in 30–60 seconds, then everyone joins in.

### 4. Extend or close (Roman vote)

When time is up, the group votes by hand signal:

- 👍 **Thumbs up:** Still hot. Extend by 2–3 minutes (once per topic).
- 👎 **Thumbs down:** Done. Move the card to "Done Topics", start the next one.
- ✊ **Sideways:** Don't mind / abstain.

Note follow-ups in "Action Items".

## 🛠️ Your role as facilitator

- **Timekeeper:** Keep the timer visible and stop consistently.
- **Visualiser:** Move cards in sync with the conversation.
- **Focus keeper:** Gently bring the group back to the card's topic.
