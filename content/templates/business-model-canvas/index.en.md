## 🚀 Business Model Canvas: How it works in 5 steps

**🎯 Purpose:** Describe, analyse or design a business model on one page, including its impact.

### 1. Define the scope (5 min)

- **Action:** Agree on the product, service or initiative you map.

### 2. Start with the customer (20 min)

- **Action:** Fill Customer Segments, then Value Propositions.
- **Rule:** One assumption per card. Be specific: who exactly, what problem?

### 3. Map delivery (20 min)

- **Action:** Fill Channels and Customer Relationships, then Revenue Streams.

### 4. Map the engine (20 min)

- **Action:** Fill Key Activities, Key Resources and Key Partners, then Cost Structure.

### 5. Check the impact (15 min)

- **Action:** Add Environmental Costs and Societal Benefits.
- **Goal:** Mark the riskiest assumptions and decide how to test them.

## 🛠️ Your role as facilitator

- **Order keeper:** Follow the sequence; it prevents jumping to costs too early.
- **Challenger:** Ask "How do we know?" for every card.
- **Synthesiser:** End with the 3 biggest risks and next tests.
