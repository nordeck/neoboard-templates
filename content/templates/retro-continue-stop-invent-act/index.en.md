## 🚀 Retrospective: How it works in 5 steps

**🎯 Purpose:** Look back on a sprint, project or event and agree on improvements.

### 1. Set the stage (5 min)

- **Action:** Name the period you are looking back on and agree to be open and respectful.

### 2. Collect (10 min, silent)

**Action:** Everyone writes cards for the quadrants:

- **CONTINUE** – what worked well and should stay?
- **STOP** – what holds us back?
- **INVENT** – what new idea would we try with unlimited resources?
- **Rule:** One point per card, use the matching colour stack.

### 3. Present and cluster (15 min)

- **Action:** Each person briefly reads out their cards. Group similar cards.

### 4. Prioritise (5 min)

- **Action:** Dot voting (e.g. 3 dots each) on the most important topics.

### 5. Act (15 min)

- **Action:** For the top topics, define concrete steps in ACT: what, who, by when.
- **Goal:** 1–3 actions that are actually done before the next retro.

## 🛠️ Your role as facilitator

- **Safe space:** Make sure nobody is blamed.
- **Timekeeper:** Protect enough time for the ACT quadrant.
- **Follow-up:** Start the next retro by checking last time's actions.
