## 🚀 SWOT Strategy: How it works in 4 steps

**🎯 Purpose:** Derive concrete strategies from a SWOT analysis.

### 1. Transfer the SWOT (10 min)

- **Action:** Place the top Strengths and Weaknesses in the "Company" rows and the top Chances and Risks in the "Environment" columns.

### 2. Combine (30 min)

**Action:** Work through the four fields:

- **DEVELOP (strengths × chances):** use strengths to seize chances.
- **PROTECT (strengths × risks):** use strengths to defend against risks.
- **REGAIN (weaknesses × chances):** use chances to overcome weaknesses.
- **AVOID (weaknesses × risks):** reduce exposure where both meet.
- **Rule:** Each strategy card names the factors it combines.

### 3. Prioritise (10 min)

- **Action:** Rate strategies by impact and effort, dot-vote the top 3.

### 4. Plan (15 min)

- **Action:** Give each top strategy an owner and a first step.

## 🛠️ Your role as facilitator

- **Connector:** Ask "Which strength helps with this chance?"
- **Realist:** Keep strategies concrete and testable.
- **Decider:** Close with clear owners.
