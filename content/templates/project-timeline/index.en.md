## 🚀 Project Timeline: How it works in 4 steps

**🎯 Purpose:** Plan a project along its milestones and make dependencies visible.

### 1. Describe the project (5 min)

- **Action:** Replace the placeholder text with goal, scope and end date.

### 2. Set milestones (15 min)

- **Action:** Rename Milestones A–F with real results and dates.
- **Rule:** A milestone is a result, not an activity ("Pilot live", not "Testing").

### 3. Add details (15 min)

- **Action:** Use the list below a milestone for tasks, owners and risks.

### 4. Check and agree (10 min)

- **Action:** Walk the timeline from left to right: is it realistic? What depends on what?
- **Goal:** A timeline everyone commits to.

## 🛠️ Your role as facilitator

- **Reality check:** Ask for buffers between milestones.
- **Clarifier:** Turn vague dates into concrete ones.
- **Keeper:** Revisit the board in each status meeting.
