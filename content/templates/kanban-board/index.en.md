## 🚀 Kanban Board: How it works in 4 steps

**🎯 Purpose:** Make work visible, limit work in progress and keep tasks flowing to Done.

### 1. Fill the backlog (10 min)

- **Action:** Write every task on its own card and place it in "To do".
- **Rule:** One task per card, phrased as an action ("Update price list").

### 2. Agree on colours and limits (5 min)

- **Action:** Assign each card colour a meaning (e.g. feature, bug, admin).
- **Rule:** Set a maximum number of cards for "In Progress" (e.g. 3 per person).

### 3. Pull work (ongoing)

- **Action:** Whoever starts a task moves the card to "In Progress" and adds their name.
- **Rule:** Only pull new work when the limit allows it.

### 4. Finish and review (weekly)

- **Action:** Move finished cards to "Done" and look at what got stuck and why.
- **Goal:** Learn where the flow slows down.

## 🛠️ Your role as facilitator

- **Flow keeper:** In short stand-ups, walk the board from right to left: what is almost done?
- **Limit guard:** Point out when "In Progress" overflows.
- **Housekeeper:** Archive old "Done" cards regularly.
