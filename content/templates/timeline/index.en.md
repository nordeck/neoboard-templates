## 🚀 Timeline: How it works in 4 steps

**🎯 Purpose:** Arrange events in time, e.g. project history, a reading unit in class or a roadmap.

### 1. Define the axis (5 min)

- **Action:** Replace the example years with your time points (years, months, sprints).

### 2. Collect events (10 min)

- **Action:** Everyone writes events on cards and places them at the right point.
- **Rule:** Above the axis = plans or milestones, below = actual events (or define your own rule).

### 3. Connect (10 min)

- **Action:** Discuss gaps, overlaps and causes. Add missing cards.

### 4. Conclude (5 min)

- **Action:** Highlight the 2–3 most important moments and what follows from them.

## 🛠️ Your role as facilitator

- **Structurer:** Agree on the above/below rule before starting.
- **Storyteller:** Summarise the timeline as a short story at the end.
