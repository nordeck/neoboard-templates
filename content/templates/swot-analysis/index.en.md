## 🚀 SWOT Analysis: How it works in 4 steps

**🎯 Purpose:** Get a shared picture of where an organisation, team or project stands.

### 1. Set the focus (5 min)

- **Action:** Name the subject of the analysis and the time horizon.

### 2. Look inside (15 min)

- **Action:** Collect Strengths (purple) and Weaknesses (cyan).
- **Rule:** Internal factors only, things you can influence.

### 3. Look outside (15 min)

- **Action:** Collect Opportunities (yellow-green) and Threats (pink).
- **Rule:** External factors only: market, technology, regulation.

### 4. Sharpen (15 min)

- **Action:** Cluster duplicates and dot-vote the 3 most important points per quadrant.
- **Goal:** A prioritised SWOT ready for the SWOT Strategy board.

## 🛠️ Your role as facilitator

- **Sorter:** Move cards that are in the wrong quadrant (internal vs. external).
- **Balance keeper:** Make sure all four quadrants get enough attention.
- **Bridge builder:** Hand the results over to strategy work.
