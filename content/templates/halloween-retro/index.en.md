## 🚀 Halloween Retro: How it works in 4 phases

**🎯 Purpose:** Look back on a sprint or project with a wink. Each phase has its own screen: from what should stop, to what works, to new ideas and finally to concrete to-dos.

### 1. Stop doing (10 min)

- **Action:** Everyone copies a rotten tomato, writes down what should stop and throws it at the grumpy pumpkin.
- **Rule:** One thought per tomato. Name habits, not people.

### 2. Proceed doing (10 min)

- **Action:** Sweet chocolate bars go to the happy pumpkin: what worked well and should continue?

### 3. Ideas (10 min)

- **Action:** Write new ideas on the idea flashes and let them fly off the witch’s broom.
- **Rule:** Collect first, judge later. Bold ideas are welcome.

### 4. To do’s (15 min)

- **Action:** Pick the most important tomatoes and ideas, e.g. by dot voting, and turn each into a to do hex in the cauldron: what, who, until when, with whom?
- **Goal:** 1–3 to-dos that are actually done before the next retro.

## 🛠️ Your role as facilitator

- **Guide:** Walk through the four screens in presentation mode; each frame is one phase.
- **Spook master:** Keep it playful without losing the serious topics.
- **Follow-up:** Start the next retro with a look at last time’s to do hexes.
